A scoreboard app for <b>Baskin</b>, the inclusive sport where people with and without disabilities play together.

The app turns an Android phone or tablet into the scorer's table: from there you control score, clock, periods, fouls and possession. The same scoreboard can be shown on a television, so the audience and both teams can follow the game.

<b>Getting the scoreboard onto a TV</b>

The app runs a small web server that works <i>on the local network only</i>: it never contacts a remote server and collects no data. A television can connect in three ways:

* using a second Android device (including a TV stick or box) running the same app in "Screen" mode, which finds the scoreboard automatically on the network;
* opening an address in the television's own browser, which the app also shows as a QR code;
* over HDMI or screen casting, using the secondary display.

<b>Features</b>

* Score, clock, periods, fouls and possession
* Roles: "Scoreboard" (controls) or "Screen" (display only)
* Works without Internet: a local Wi-Fi network is enough
* No ads, no tracking, no data collection
* Interface in Italian, English and French
* Works on Android TV and on boxes/sticks with no touchscreen
